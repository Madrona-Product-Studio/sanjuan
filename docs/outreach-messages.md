## 3. San Juan Sailing / San Juan Yachting (Bellingham)

_The #1 bareboat charter company on the West Coast. Every single customer sails the San Juans. This is the highest-value outreach._

**Subject:** Free cruising guide for your charter guests

**Message:**

Hi — I've spent the past few years cruising the San Juan Islands and taking notes on every marina, dock, and anchorage I've visited. I finally turned those notes into a free web-based guide built for on-the-water use.

It covers 40+ moorage locations with VHF channels, fuel, and rates. Walk-from-dock distances to restaurants. Marine park details — which bays have buoys, which ones fill early, what hazards to watch for. Plus live NOAA conditions with wind, tides, and marine forecasts.

I built it because this is the kind of information I wished I'd had when I started cruising these waters, and I think your charter guests would find it useful too.

**sjiboating.com**

Happy to share however works best for your team — a link in your pre-departure packet, on your website, or just passing it along. I'd also welcome any feedback from your crew.
