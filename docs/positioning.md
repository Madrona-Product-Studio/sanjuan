# Positioning — San Juan Islands Boating Guide

_madrona-gtm Phase 1 one-pager. Meta descriptions and outreach messages should lift lines from this verbatim. Numbers verified against the built sitemap (149 pages) and `data/` on 2026-08-17._

## Who it's for

Visiting boaters cruising the San Juan Islands — charter guests, boat-club members, and owners — arriving by boat and planning from the helm or the dock.

## The problem

The details that actually matter on the water — VHF channels, fuel availability, walk-from-dock distances to restaurants, which bays fill up by noon on weekends — are the kind of local knowledge that's hard to find online, and hard to check from a phone in a rolling anchorage.

## What they use today instead

Paper cruising guides like the Waggoner, a scatter of individual marina and parks websites, and dock talk — none of it mobile-first, cross-linked, or paired with live conditions.

## Why this is different

It's one free, mobile-friendly guide built for use on the water, written from firsthand notes taken at every dock — everything oriented around arriving by boat, with live NOAA wind, tides, and marine forecasts built in. No ads, no paywall.

## The proof

149 pages covering 41 marinas and moorage locations (including 24 state-park anchorages and buoy fields), 10 marine parks with buoy counts and hazards, 13 islands, 29 restaurants with walk-from-dock distances, 29 trails, 10 farms, and 12 culture stops — every one visited by the author over several summers of cruising, live at [sjiboating.com](https://www.sjiboating.com).

## One-line pitch

> A free, phone-first cruising guide to the San Juan Islands — every marina, anchorage, and dock, with the local knowledge you only learn by being there, plus live NOAA conditions.
